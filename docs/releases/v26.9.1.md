## WiVRn for macOS 26.9.1

- **Clean shutdown**: quitting WiVRn.app through a signal (SIGTERM, SIGINT, SIGHUP) now stops the server and the
  private D-Bus it started, and a watchdog stops them if the dashboard dies. Before, they could be left running.
- **Update checks** are logged in `~/Library/Logs/WiVRn` (current, available, or the error).
